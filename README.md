## Product Overview

Elyon Seller Tool consolidates key e-commerce tasks in a web-based seller dashboard. Sellers can investigate and assess products, prepare eBay listings, handle orders and shipping information, manage invoices and returns, and connect workflows with CJdropshipping.

Its connected workflow covers product research, listing operations, fulfillment, and seller record management. OpenAI and DeepSeek integrations can assist with product evaluation and keyword creation, while Google Sheets synchronization and backup import/export help keep data organized across workflows and environments.

## Typical Workflow

A common operating sequence looks like this:

1. Launch the dashboard in a supported modern browser.
2. Investigate and evaluate products with the available research tools.
3. Apply AI-assisted analysis and keyword generation while preparing product details.
4. Authorize eBay through OAuth for account or listing operations.
5. Build or refine an eBay listing.
6. Coordinate product and order details through CJdropshipping.
7. Handle shipping information, invoices, and returns in the relevant management sections.
8. Send applicable records to Google Sheets.
9. Export a backup to preserve the current data set or move it to another environment.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What type of seller can use Elyon Seller Tool?

The platform is built for e-commerce sellers handling product research, eBay listings, order coordination, shipping tasks, and associated seller records.

### Is eBay supported?

Yes. Elyon Seller Tool supports eBay OAuth connections and includes workflows for generating and optimizing listings.

### Does the platform integrate with CJdropshipping?

Yes. CJdropshipping product and order workflows are supported.

### What do the AI tools do?

Configured OpenAI or DeepSeek services can provide assistance with product analysis and keyword generation.

### Is Google Sheets integration available?

Yes. Supported seller data can be synchronized with Google Sheets. Using this capability may require a Google account and the appropriate connection setup.

### What can I do when an integration fails?

Check the service credentials and connection settings first. Also confirm that the connected account has the required permissions, then inspect browser or deployment logs for connection-related errors.

### How should I back up seller data?

Export backups regularly and store the resulting files somewhere appropriate. Supported data can later be restored or moved by importing those backups.

### Where do updates appear?

New versions may be provided through the hosted build or the project repository. Review the latest available build before beginning another deployment.
